# Controlled Web-Product Delivery - Sanitized Case Study

## Problem

Business and human-service ideas usually arrive as loose requirements, conversations, forms, and operational assumptions rather than a clean software specification. My role has been to convert that ambiguity into structured product behavior that can be built, tested, reviewed, and released in controlled stages.

## My role

Co-founder / AI product operations lead across private bilingual web-product work. I translate domain-owner intent into product journeys, schemas, permissions, acceptance criteria, QA plans, and release boundaries, then coordinate AI-assisted implementation and independent review.

## Delivery pattern

```text
Domain problem
  -> current-state mapping
  -> user journeys + data model
  -> permissions / risk boundaries
  -> acceptance criteria
  -> AI-assisted implementation
  -> functional + negative testing
  -> review / remediation
  -> controlled rollout
```

## Technical and product surfaces

- Bilingual web-product requirements and structured user journeys.
- SQL-backed product data and Supabase-backed workflows.
- Vercel-based web delivery in selected projects.
- Forms, dashboards, permissions, operator/admin views, and review queues.
- Explicit privacy/safety boundaries for sensitive workflows.
- QA criteria, issue remediation, test evidence, and controlled release gates.
- Human ownership of consequential business logic and release decisions.

## AI-native working method

I use AI coding and reasoning tools to accelerate research, architecture exploration, implementation, debugging, test creation, documentation, and review. I do not treat the model output as evidence of correctness; acceptance is tied to observable product behavior, tests, and independent review.

## What this demonstrates

- Product judgment across ambiguous requirements rather than only pre-specified tickets.
- Ability to move between product, operations, technical implementation, QA, and release decisions.
- Rapid prototyping while preserving explicit limits and rollback/remediation paths.
- Experience coordinating AI-assisted builders with domain owners and reviewers.

## Public boundary

The underlying product repositories and user data are private. This case study intentionally omits customer data, private screenshots, credentials, internal URLs, and unpublished product details. A controlled review can be arranged when appropriate.
